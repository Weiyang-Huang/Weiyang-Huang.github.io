Structured Multimodal & Spatial Reasoning

Can a model build a structured picture of a scene — and reason over it?

I am interested in multimodal systems that build structured representations of scenes and reason over objects, attributes, and relations. I hope to study how object-centric and spatial representations can support reliable reasoning across images, video, 3D environments, and other sensory modalities.

Diagnostic Evaluation & Evidence Grounding

Is the answer actually grounded in the evidence that supports it?

I am interested in whether multimodal models actually rely on the visual, spatial, and cross-modal evidence behind their predictions. I hope to develop controlled and counterfactual evaluations that reveal failures in grounding, spatial reasoning, and evidence use — distinguishing sensitivity to relevant changes from robustness to irrelevant ones.

  • counterfactual
  • grounding
  • robustness
  • diagnostics
